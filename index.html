Frequently Asked Questions

In SSC CGL, percentile is calculated using the formula: Percentile = (Number of candidates who scored less than you ÷ Total candidates) × 100. For example, if 9,50,000 out of 10,00,000 candidates scored below you, your percentile is 95. CrackCalc's Score to Percentile calculator uses historical SSC CGL cut-offs to estimate your percentile and selection chances instantly.

UPSC Civil Services Preliminary exam deducts 1/3 mark (0.33) for every wrong answer in GS Paper 1 and Paper 2 (CSAT). So if each question carries 2 marks, you lose 0.66 marks per wrong answer. Use the CrackCalc Negative Marking Calculator to find your exact net score — enter total attempted, correct answers, and select the -1/3 marking scheme.

The age limit for IBPS PO is 20–30 years for General category candidates. OBC candidates get 3 years relaxation (20–33 years), SC/ST candidates get 5 years (20–35 years), and PwBD candidates get 10 years (20–40 years). Use CrackCalc's Age Eligibility Checker — enter your date of birth and category to instantly see all exams you qualify for.

After 7th Pay Commission, an SSC CGL Group B officer (Inspector/Examiner level) typically earns ₹44,900–₹1,42,400 in Pay Level 7. With HRA (24% in X-cities), DA, and Transport Allowance, the gross monthly salary is approximately ₹55,000–₹65,000 in metro cities. Use CrackCalc's Government Salary Calculator to get a city-wise, post-wise salary breakdown in seconds.

Most SSC CGL toppers recommend 6–8 hours of focused study per day for a 6-month preparation window. However, the ideal daily hours depend on how far your exam date is and how much of the syllabus is remaining. Use CrackCalc's Exam Countdown Planner — enter your exam date and syllabus completion percentage to get a customised daily study hours target with weekly milestones.

Based on historical trends, the expected IBPS PO Prelims cut-off for General category is 55–65 out of 100. OBC cut-off is typically 50–60, and SC/ST cut-off is around 42–52. These vary each year based on exam difficulty, vacancy count, and total applicants. Use CrackCalc's Cut-off Safety Meter to see how safe your score is relative to historical cut-offs for your category.
